Wedding invitation template FAQ

How many wedding invitation templates does ChungDoi have?

ChungDoi offers a wide variety of online wedding invitation templates in many styles: traditional (Double Dragon, Dragon Phoenix), nature-inspired (Spring Garden), modern (Elegant Leaf), romantic (Cherry Blossom), and fun (Chibi). Each template comes in multiple color variants, and new designs are added regularly.

How do I choose the right template for my wedding?

Consider your wedding style: choose Double Dragon or Dragon Phoenix for a traditional ceremony, Spring Garden for a garden party, Elegant Leaf for a modern celebration, Cherry Blossom for romance, or Chibi for a playful vibe. You can preview a live demo of each template before deciding.

Can I switch templates after creating my invitation?

Yes! You can change your template anytime from the dashboard. All your content (names, dates, venues, photos) is preserved when you switch to a new design.

Are all templates free to try?

Yes. Every template on ChungDoi is free to create and comes with a 3-day full-feature trial. You only pay when you are truly satisfied.

Which wedding invitation style should I choose?

Start with the overall theme rather than one specific template. Based on ChungDoi invitation data from Q1 2026, traditional designs were the most selected style, followed by nature-inspired designs, then modern and minimalist designs. Traditional styles work well for family-centered ceremonies, nature-inspired templates suit garden or outdoor weddings, and minimalist templates fit modern venues or destination weddings.
